Usage guidelines, trademark notice, icon credits, and how to request removal.

How these icons are sourced

Each SVG icon in this library is sourced from the official brand’s website or press kit. Where possible we link back to the brand’s own asset page so you can verify the latest version. We do not create or modify brand logos — we simply collect and organise what is publicly available.

Trademark notice

All brand names, logos, and trademarks are the property of their respective owners. The icons in this library are provided as reference assets for identification purposes only. Use of a logo does not imply endorsement by or affiliation with the brand owner.

If you are a brand owner and would like your logo removed, please see the removal section below.

Usage

Do I need permission to use a brand logo?

Yes, in most cases. Brand logos are trademarked assets. You should check the brand’s own brand guidelines or contact them directly before using their logo, especially for commercial purposes. This library does not grant you any rights to use the logos.

Can I use these icons in my app or website?

It depends on the brand and your use case. Some brands allow the use of their logo for reference or attribution purposes (e.g. “Built with X” or a partner badge). Others require written permission. When in doubt, reach out to the brand.

What about open-source projects?

Even for open-source projects, brand logos are typically governed by trademark law. A permissive open-source license on the code does not automatically cover the use of a brand’s logo. Always check the brand’s policy.

Removal requests

If you are a brand owner or representative and would like your logo removed from this library, please open an issue on GitHub. We review and process removal requests promptly.

Open a removal issue on GitHub

We aim to respond within a few days. You can also reach out via the contact channels listed on the Reicon.dev website.

Credit

This library is maintained by Reicon.dev. Most icons are sourced directly from brand press kits, official brand asset pages, or open-source icon collections that distribute under permissive terms.

If you use this library, no attribution to Reicon.dev is required — we are simply a curator. However, we encourage you to respect the original brand’s guidelines and give credit where the brand asks for it.

Accuracy

We try to keep icons up to date, but brands refresh their identities over time. If you notice an outdated logo or incorrect brand colour, feel free to open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
